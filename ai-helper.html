FOLIO RESUME STUDIO · YOUR STORY, YOURS

AI resume helper

Bring your own AI assistant into the writing process. Folio gives you a backup, a facts-only prompt, and the exact format to return your changes safely.

  1. 01

    Back up your resume

    Open the editor and choose Backup. Keep the downloaded JSON file somewhere you can find it.

    Open editor ↗
  2. 02

    Edit with AI

    Give your backup and the prompt below to an AI assistant you trust. Review its edits before bringing the file back.

    The prompt asks for writing improvements while keeping your existing facts and backup data intact.

  3. 03

    Return and import

    Save the assistant’s result as a .json file. In Folio, choose Import backup and select it. The editor’s own validation is authoritative; invalid files are rejected before they can change your resume. Undo is available for resume changes.

    Return to editor ↗
