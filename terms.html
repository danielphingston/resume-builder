Terms of use

Folio lets you create, edit, and export resumes in your browser, with optional saves to your Google Drive.

Last updated September 15, 2026

Your content

You control the information you enter and the files you export or save to Drive. Folio does not claim ownership of your resume content. Please enter information you have the right to use and review a resume before sharing it.

Backups and accuracy

Browser storage can be cleared or lost. Keep a backup or a Drive copy of work you need to retain. Folio's resume checker is a writing and formatting aid; it does not guarantee how an employer or applicant tracking system will assess a resume. Review exported PDFs before sending them.

Google Drive

Drive access is optional and begins only after you approve Google's authorization prompt. Your use of Google Drive is also governed by Google's terms. You may disconnect Folio or remove its access through your Google Account settings. Folio uses the file-level permission described in the privacy policy.

Availability and contact

Folio is provided as a browser-based tool without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability or a particular hiring outcome. For support questions, email danielphingston@proton.me.
